Almost there – connect Firebase

This app needs a free Firebase project to store companies, drivers and routes.

  1. Create the project (see README.md, section 1).
  2. Open js/firebase-config.js and paste in your project's values.
  3. Reload this page.

Just want to look around first? If you are running this on your own computer, open http://localhost:5000/?emulator=1 after running npm run demo (see README, section 3).

Apply to existing routes

Saving only changes the company default and routes you create or import afterwards. To put the saved details on routes you already have, choose how and press the button.

Add a driver or maintenance login

This creates a login. Give them the email and temporary password; they can change it with “Forgot password?” on the sign-in screen. A maintenance login only sees vehicle defects reported by drivers (no routes, runs or settings).
